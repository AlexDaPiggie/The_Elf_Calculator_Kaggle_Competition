## 2. Problem-class structure (new, checkable)
- Hansen PROGRESS.md: on scored_0006 four integers (261,389 / 327,190 / 571,027 / 835,418) explain all 32 targets.
  Verified here: small targets = x_i*x_j + r with r 1-9 bits; big ones = q*x_i + r. A plain scan (h in 2^14..2^21,
  count targets > 2^32 with |T mod h| < h/512) finds all 3 top hubs from targets alone (13/12/11 hits; noise max ~4-6)
  and all 3 are values in our best program. Scan over all 1000 puzzles (8 min numpy): max-hit histogram mostly 3-5
  (noise); 103 puzzles >= 7 hits, 69 >= 8, 33 >= 10 (max 18: scored_0904, 0567). Best already cheaper there (4.48
  instr/target vs 5.93 rest), but the exact top hub is a value in best for only 41/103 (32/69 at >= 8). E.g. 0904
  (18 hits, hub 269,928 absent, 117 instr), 0567, 0082, 0575, 0590, 0436 = test set for idea 3.
- Rudolph oracle: told the hubs of the best program, his solver cuts 16 cracked puzzles 2,900 -> 2,330 (-20%).
  His detector (divisor near 3+ targets, forced as targets) failed on uncracked puzzles (4,718 -> 4,840): forced
  wrong hubs. Hubs median 18 bits, quotient 23, remainder 10.
